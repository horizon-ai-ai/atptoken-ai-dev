Name

What's in the name

The full name is Agent Token Petrichor. Two of the three words describe how the platform works — who is spending, and what it is denominated in. The third is simply where the name came from.

  1. AAgent

    Who spends

    Requests no longer come from a person at a keyboard. They come from agents that loop, retry, and call tools on their own — which is exactly why spending needs a structure that does not depend on someone remembering to check.

  2. TToken

    What it is denominated in

    Text models are metered in tokens; other models use their published image, second or character rates. ATP records each request under its project and settles usage in credits.

  3. PPetrichor

    Where the name came from

    Petrichor is a rare English word, coined from Greek roots (petra + ichor). Here it is only etymology — where the letters came from — not a product metaphor and not part of the brand promise. What the product does is plain: compute becomes action that is attributed and accounted for. The full name lives on in our legal entity and in older material; the product is ATP Token.

The initials are not a coincidence. In biology, ATP is the cell's energy currency: whatever the source, energy has to be converted into ATP before the body can spend it. Compute works the same way — it has to become tokens before an enterprise can actually use it. That conversion, metered and accounted for, is the layer we work at.
