Meet Horizon AI
The name

Agent · Token · Petrichor

The full name is Agent Token Petrichor. Three words, because the platform has to answer three questions at once: who is spending, what it is denominated in, and why any of it matters.

The initials are not a coincidence. In biology, ATP is the cell's energy currency: whatever the source, energy has to be converted into ATP before the body can spend it. Compute works the same way — it has to become tokens before an enterprise can actually use it. That conversion, metered and accounted for, is the layer we work at.

AgentWho spends

Requests no longer come from a person at a keyboard. They come from agents that loop, retry, and call tools on their own — which is exactly why spending needs a structure that does not depend on someone remembering to check.

TokenWhat it is denominated in

Tokens are the unit intelligence is actually priced in. Every request is metered in them, attributed to a project, and settled in credits — so cost stops being a monthly surprise and becomes a number per call.

PetrichorWhy it matters

Petrichor is the scent of earth after rain. New models keep falling like that rain, landing in every corner of an organization. Our work is to channel that rain rather than let it pool where nobody is looking.
