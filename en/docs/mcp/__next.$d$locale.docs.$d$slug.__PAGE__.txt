<ol>
<li>Open <strong>Customize → Connectors</strong>.</li>
<li>Click <strong>+ Add</strong>, then <strong>Add custom connector</strong>.</li>
<li>Name it <code>ATP Token</code>, paste <code>https://mcp.atptoken.ai/mcp</code> as the URL, and click <strong>Continue</strong>.</li>
<li>Under <strong>Authentication</strong>, choose <strong>No sign in</strong>. To use the live model list, add a request header <code>Authorization</code> with the value <code>Bearer atp-…</code>.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Add</strong>, then turn the connector on in a new chat.</li>
</ol>
<p>On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner adds it under <strong><a class="doc-term" href="/docs/console-setup/" data-def="The top level of an account. Workspaces and projects sit underneath it.">Organization</a> settings → Connectors</strong>, and members then click <strong>Connect</strong>. The desktop app&#39;s <code>claude_desktop_config.json</code> does not accept a URL; use Connectors instead.</p>
<h3 data-md="h3" id="chatgpt" class="group">ChatGPT<a href="#chatgpt" aria-label="Link to this section" class="ml-2 text-sand opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100 focus-visible:opacity-100 touch:opacity-60">#</a></h3>
<ol>
<li>In <strong>Settings → Apps &amp; Connectors → Advanced settings</strong>, turn on <strong>Developer mode</strong>.</li>
<li>Back in <strong>Apps &amp; Connectors</strong>, click <strong>Create</strong>.</li>
<li>Name it <code>ATP Token</code>, paste <code>https://mcp.atptoken.ai/mcp</code> as the URL, and choose <strong>No authentication</strong>.</li>
<li>Turn it on in a new chat from the tools menu.</li>
</ol>
<p>Menu names and which plans can add custom connectors change from time to time; if you do not see <strong>Developer mode</strong>, check OpenAI&#39;s help for your plan.</p>
