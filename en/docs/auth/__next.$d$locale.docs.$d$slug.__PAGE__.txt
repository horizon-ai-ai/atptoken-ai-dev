<h2 data-md="h2" id="get-a-key-first" class="group">Get a key first<a href="#get-a-key-first" aria-label="Link to this section" class="ml-2 text-sand opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100 focus-visible:opacity-100 touch:opacity-60">#</a></h2>
