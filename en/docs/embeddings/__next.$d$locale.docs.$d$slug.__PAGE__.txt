<p>Embeddings bill on input tokens only. For the Gemini ids the token count follows the content: text by tokens, image at 258 tokens per image, audio at 25 tokens per second, and video at 66 tokens per frame at one frame per second. A content element with several parts bills the sum of its parts. Every element shares one 8,192-token window, and content past it is dropped rather than refused. How tokens turn into <a class="doc-term" href="/docs/credits/" data-def="The billing unit. 1 USD = 100 credits; usage converts at each model's list rate.">credits</a> is on <a href="/docs/credits/">Credits</a>.</p>
