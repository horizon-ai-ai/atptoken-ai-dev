<h2 data-md="h2" id="choose-a-model" class="group">Choose a model<a href="#choose-a-model" aria-label="Link to this section" class="ml-2 text-sand opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100 focus-visible:opacity-100 touch:opacity-60">#</a></h2>

<h3 data-md="h3" id="wrong-field-names-do-not-raise-errors" class="group">Wrong field names do not raise errors<a href="#wrong-field-names-do-not-raise-errors" aria-label="Link to this section" class="ml-2 text-sand opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100 focus-visible:opacity-100 touch:opacity-60">#</a></h3>
<p>The <code>content[]</code> families <strong>do not check field names</strong>. A misspelled field, or one the model does not read, still gets <code>202</code> and simply has no effect. Undeclared top-level fields on Kling and Seedance are forwarded to the model as-is (for example <code>negative_prompt</code>); fields placed in an <code>input: {…}</code> bag are forwarded too, and <code>input</code> wins when names clash.</p>
<p>To confirm a parameter took effect, read <code>resolved_params</code> (the parameters the model actually used) in the polled task; <code>params_raw</code> is the body the gateway sent. <code>resolved_params</code> also includes defaults the model filled in, so compare the <strong>value</strong> with what you sent, not just whether the field is present.</p>
<p>The flat family is the opposite: extra fields are rejected with <code>400 Extra inputs are not permitted</code>.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>The order is fixed.</strong> You cannot register without a <code>group_id</code>. <li><strong>Create only makes a light reservation</strong>: any positive balance passes; only an exhausted balance (<code>credit − inflight ≤ 0</code>) returns <code>402</code>.</li>
<li>If the balance is short at settlement it goes negative, and <strong>the next request is blocked</strong>.</li>
<li>Gating decides who can use a model, not what it costs; ungated models are billed the same way.</li>
<li>Requests rejected at create create no task and are not billed. Failed tasks are not billed.</li>
<li>Models that take an input video (for example <code>-r2v</code>, <code>-video-edit</code>) <strong>also bill the input seconds</strong>: a 5-second source producing a 5-second clip bills 10 seconds.</li>
