<h2 id="faqs">FAQs</h2>
<h3 id="what-is-shadow-ai">What is shadow AI?</h3>
<p>Shadow AI is employees or systems using AI tools, models, or API keys outside the company&#39;s approved identity, procurement, data handling, and logging controls.</p>
<h3 id="why-is-shadow-ai-a-risk">Why is shadow AI a risk?</h3>
<p>It creates data leakage paths, unowned spend on personal cards, keys that never revoke on offboarding, and zero audit trail when something goes wrong.</p>
<h3 id="does-banning-ai-tools-stop-shadow-ai">Does banning AI tools stop shadow AI?</h3>
<p>Usually no. People route around blocks with personal devices and accounts. The durable fix is an approved path that is easier and safer than the shadow path.</p>
<h3 id="how-do-i-reduce-shadow-ai-without-slowing-teams">How do I reduce shadow AI without slowing teams?</h3>
<p>Provide project-scoped keys quickly, clear model allowlists, sandbox budgets, and SDK-compatible gateways so approved usage is the path of least resistance.</p>
<h3 id="where-do-leaked-api-keys-fit-in">Where do leaked API keys fit in?</h3>
<p>Keys committed to git or embedded in apps are a shadow-adjacent failure: spend and abuse happen outside ops visibility until the invoice or a fraud alert arrives.</p>
