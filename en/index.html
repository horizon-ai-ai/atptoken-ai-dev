01 / Governance

Draw the lines once. Every request stays inside them.

Your organization holds the credits. Workspaces split them by team, projects by product, and each project gets its own budget and model allowlist. When a project reaches its limit, it stops there and nowhere else.

  • OrganizationOne credit wallet, one bill, members and roles.
  • WorkspaceA team or department, with credits allocated from the organization.
  • ProjectA product or cost center: its own budget, model allowlist and keys.

Illustration: an organization split into two workspaces and four projects. Each request fills one pixel of a project's budget; when the video project reaches its limit, its requests are refused while the other projects keep running.
