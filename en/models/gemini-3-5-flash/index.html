Gemini 3.5 Flash is Google's generally available Flash-tier Gemini model, launched in May 2026 for agentic and coding work at Flash speed. It reads text, images, video, audio and PDFs, defaults to medium thinking effort, and carries its reasoning across multi-turn conversations automatically. Google has since released Gemini 3.6 Flash as a more token-efficient follow-up.

Where it fits

Good fit
  • Iterative coding loops, rapid prototyping, and sub-agent fan-out
  • Multi-step tool-calling workflows that run for many turns
  • Multimodal pipelines mixing text, image, video, audio, and PDF inputs
  • Long-document review and chart reading over a 1M-token context
Usually not ideal
  • Questions about events after January 2025 without search grounding (Google's stated knowledge cutoff)
  • New agent builds chasing token efficiency — Google positions Gemini 3.6 Flash (also on ATP) as leaner

FAQ

4
Can Gemini 3.5 Flash use tools, read images, or think before answering?
Tool calling: yes. Vision input: yes. Extended thinking: yes. Audio: yes. These come from the ATP model catalog, so check with your key before you rely on one.
How much context does Gemini 3.5 Flash take?
Google lists a 1,048,576-token context window for Gemini 3.5 Flash. Source: Google AI for Developers — Gemini 3.5 Flash (checked 2026-10-01). The model list your key can see shows the limit you actually get.
How is Gemini 3.5 Flash priced?
You pay per 1M input tokens and per 1M output tokens. You only pay when a request succeeds. $1 = 100 credits. See pricing
Is there a newer Google model than Gemini 3.5 Flash on ATP?
Yes. Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite (gemini-3-5-flash-lite) and Gemini 3.6 Flash (gemini-3-6-flash) came out on Jul 21, 2026, after Gemini 3.5 Flash (May 19, 2026), and are the newer Google text option on ATP. Both stay available: compare family prices on this page, then turn on the id you want for your project.
