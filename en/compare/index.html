03 / Method

How we compare

  • Prices, capabilities and context come from the ATP model catalog at build time — the same data as the model price table.
  • Context is shown only where a primary source documents it; otherwise the cell says “—”.
  • Claims beyond our data cite the vendor or a named third party.
  • Video samples are real outputs from the same word-for-word prompt; each clip states the resolution and duration it actually produced.
