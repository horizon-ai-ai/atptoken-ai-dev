Fish Audio S2 Pro is Fish Audio's openly released text-to-speech model, trained on more than 10 million hours of audio across 80+ languages. Free-form inline tags such as [whisper] or [excited] steer emotion and prosody word by word, and it handles multi-speaker, multi-turn dialogue. Fish Audio now lists it as its previous-generation S2 model, succeeded by S2.1-Pro.

Where it fits

Good fit
  • Expressive narration and character voices steered with inline emotion tags
  • Multi-speaker dialogue such as podcast-style or drama scripts
  • Multilingual voiceover across Chinese, English, Japanese and dozens of other languages
  • Budgets that track script length, since ATP bills per input character
Usually not ideal
  • Speech recognition or audio understanding — this id is text-to-speech only
  • Commercial self-hosting of the open weights — they ship under a research, non-commercial license

FAQ

3
What can I send Fish S2 Pro, and what do I get back?
Fish S2 Pro is a Fish Audio text-to-speech model. Send the text to speak and you get back speech audio.
How is Fish S2 Pro priced?
You pay per 1,000 characters of text you send. You only pay when a request succeeds. $1 = 100 credits. See pricing
How do I call Fish S2 Pro on ATP?
Create a project API key. Check that "fish-s2-pro" shows up with GET https://api.atptoken.ai/v1/models, then follow the speech generation docs with model set to that id.
